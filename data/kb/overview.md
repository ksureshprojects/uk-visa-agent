# Standard Visitor Visa — Overview

The Standard Visitor visa is the route used to come to the UK for short-term visits, including tourism, seeing family or friends, business activities, short-term study (courses of up to 6 months), and private medical treatment. It replaced the separate "Business Visitor", "Family Visitor" and "General Visitor" visa categories, which were merged into this single Standard Visitor category.

Key facts:

- **Maximum stay per visit**: up to 6 months, regardless of which visa validity you hold.
- **Visa cost**: £135 for a visa valid for up to 6 months (see `fees-and-processing.md` for the full fee table and the caveat on how current that figure is).
- **Where to apply from**: outside the UK, generally from the country where you live or have lawful residence. You cannot switch into the Standard Visitor route from inside the UK in most circumstances.
- **Not a route to settlement**: time spent on a Standard Visitor visa does not count towards indefinite leave to remain, and the route does not permit marriage/civil partnership (a separate Marriage Visitor visa exists for that), long-term study, or work.
- **Who needs this visa**: nationals of countries that require a visa to visit the UK (as opposed to being eligible for an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or having no pre-travel permission requirement at all). Applicants should check `gov.uk/check-uk-visa` to confirm whether they need a Standard Visitor visa specifically, since ETA is a separate, lighter-weight authorisation for nationals who do not need a full visa.

This visa is governed by **Immigration Rules Appendix Visitor** (the current rules), which replaced the older "Appendix V: Visitor". Both names appear in Home Office guidance; treat "Appendix Visitor" as the live, current version.
