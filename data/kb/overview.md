# Standard Visitor Visa — Overview

Visitors may come to the UK for "tourism, business, study (courses up to 6 months) and other permitted activities." This route replaced the older separate "Business Visitor", "Family Visitor" and "General Visitor" visa categories, which were merged into this single Standard Visitor category.

Key facts:

- **Maximum stay per visit**: up to 6 months, regardless of which visa validity you hold (potential extensions apply only in specific circumstances, e.g. private medical treatment — see `application-process.md`).
- **Visa cost**: £135 for a visa valid for up to 6 months (see `fees-and-processing.md` for the full fee table, including long-term and priority-service figures).
- **Where to apply from**: outside the UK, generally from the country where you live or have lawful residence. You cannot switch into the Standard Visitor route from inside the UK in most circumstances.
- **Not a route to settlement**: time spent on a Standard Visitor visa does not count towards indefinite leave to remain, and the route does not permit marriage/civil partnership registration (a separate Marriage/Civil Partnership Visitor category exists for that), long-term study, or work.
- **Who needs this visa**: nationals of countries that require a visa to visit the UK, as opposed to being eligible for an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) or having no pre-travel permission requirement at all. gov.uk directs applicants to its visa checker tool to confirm which of these applies to them; even visa-exempt visitors must still meet the eligibility requirements and may be questioned about this at the UK border.

## What you need to do

Per gov.uk's stated process: verify your planned activities qualify as Standard Visitor activities; confirm you meet the eligibility requirements (`eligibility.md`); determine whether you need a visa or an ETA for your nationality; and apply online if a visa is required.

## Permitted and prohibited activities (summary)

This is a high-level summary; see `permitted-activities.md` and `prohibited-activities.md` for the full detail.

- **Permitted**: tourism/holidays, visiting family or friends, volunteering with a registered charity for up to 30 days in total, transiting through the UK, certain business activities (meetings, interviews, conferences), Permitted Paid Engagements as a professional expert, school exchange participation, recreational courses of up to 30 days, short study/placements/exams, academic/senior doctor/dentist visits, and medical treatment visits.
- **Prohibited**: paid or unpaid work (except Permitted Paid Engagements), claiming public funds, living in the UK for long periods through frequent or successive visits, and marrying or registering a civil partnership.

## Related routes

- **Transit only**: a visitor only passing through the UK may instead apply for a **Visitor in Transit visa**, which currently costs **£74.50** rather than the £135 Standard Visitor fee.
- **Long-term visitor visas**: frequent visitors can apply for 2, 5, or 10-year Standard Visitor visas; each individual visit is still capped at 6 months (see `fees-and-processing.md` for pricing).

This visa is governed by **Immigration Rules Appendix V: Visitor**. gov.uk's live guidance page uses this "Appendix V: Visitor" name; an "Appendix Visitor" URL was tried directly during this KB refresh and returned a 404, so "Appendix V: Visitor" is confirmed as the current, correct name of the guidance (superseding the earlier assumption in prior KB versions that "Appendix Visitor" had replaced it).
