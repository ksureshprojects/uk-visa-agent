# Student Visa — Financial Requirement

## Monthly living-cost figures

- **Studying in London**: "£1,529 per month (for up to 9 months)"
- **Studying outside London**: "£1,171 per month (for up to 9 months)"

These cover a maximum of 9 months regardless of course length — course fees for the full academic year (up to 9 months, per the CAS) must be shown separately, on top of these living-cost figures.

## How funds must be held

Applicants must show the required amount was held "for at least 28 days in a row," and "the end date of the 28-day period must be within 31 days of the date you apply for your visa" — i.e. the 28-day bank statement window must be recent relative to the application date, not an old snapshot.

## Course fees

Applicants must be able to cover "your course for 1 academic year (up to 9 months)," using the fee amount shown on their CAS as the reference figure — if a deposit or full fee has already been paid to the sponsor, evidence of that payment can offset the amount that still needs to be shown as available funds (confirm current paid-amount evidence with the specific sponsor/CAS).

## Loans and sponsorship

"If you have a student loan or financial sponsorship, you'll need to provide evidence of this from your loan or sponsorship company" — this can be used instead of personal bank statements to meet some or all of the financial requirement.
