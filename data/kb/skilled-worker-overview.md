# Skilled Worker Visa — Overview

"A Skilled Worker visa allows you to come to or stay in the UK to do an eligible job with an approved employer." This route replaced the older Tier 2 (General) work visa.

Key facts:

- **Visa duration**: "Your visa can last for up to 5 years before you need to extend it."
- **Settlement**: after 5 years of continuous residence on this route, applicants may be eligible to apply for indefinite leave to remain (ILR), which gives "the right to live, work and study here for as long as you like."
- **Dependants**: "Your partner and children may be able to apply to join you" as dependants.
- **Restrictions while on this visa**: holders "cannot apply for most benefits (public funds), or the State Pension," and cannot change employer or job without getting the visa updated first (a new certificate of sponsorship and, in most cases, a new application is needed to switch sponsors or roles).
- **Core requirements at a glance**: a confirmed job offer from a Home Office-approved (licensed) UK sponsor, a certificate of sponsorship, an eligible occupation, a minimum salary, and proof of English language ability — see `skilled-worker-eligibility-01` for detail.

See `skilled-worker-fees-01` for costs, `skilled-worker-required-documents-01` for what to provide, and `skilled-worker-application-process-01` for how to apply.
