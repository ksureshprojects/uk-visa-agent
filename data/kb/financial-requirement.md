# Standard Visitor Visa — Financial / Maintenance Requirement

## No fixed minimum amount
Unlike some other UK visa routes (e.g. Student, Skilled Worker), the Standard Visitor route has **no fixed minimum bank balance or published financial threshold**. There is no gov.uk-published "you must show £X" figure for this route.

## What must instead be shown
The applicant must satisfy the decision maker that they:
- Can **support themselves and any dependants** for the whole of the planned visit, **or**
- Have **funding from someone else** (a sponsor/host) who will support them, and

either way, have access to **sufficient resources to maintain and accommodate themselves adequately** for the entire length of the planned stay, covering:
- Day-to-day living costs during the visit.
- Accommodation costs (unless being hosted for free, in which case this should be evidenced instead — see `required-documents.md`).
- The cost of the **return or onward journey**.
- Costs relating to any **dependants** travelling with them.
- The cost of any **planned paid activity**, e.g. private medical treatment, course fees for short study, etc.

## Without working or accessing public funds
Critically, the funds relied on must **not** come from working in the UK (which is prohibited — see `prohibited-activities.md`) or from UK public funds/benefits (also prohibited).

## Third-party sponsorship
If someone else (a UK-based friend, relative, or a host organisation) is funding or hosting the visit, the application should evidence:
- The relationship between the applicant and the sponsor/host.
- The sponsor/host's own ability to fund/host (e.g. their own bank statements, payslips, or proof of accommodation).
- A clear statement of exactly what costs the sponsor/host is covering (all costs vs partial).

## Gap / uncertainty flagged
Because there is no published numeric threshold, the assessment is inherently case-by-case and judgment-based by the caseworker, based on the applicant's stated itinerary, length of stay, and accommodation arrangements. This knowledge base cannot and should not invent a specific number (e.g. "£X per day") — no such official figure exists for this route, and any third-party website quoting a specific minimum balance for the Standard Visitor visa should be treated as informal guidance, not an official gov.uk requirement.
