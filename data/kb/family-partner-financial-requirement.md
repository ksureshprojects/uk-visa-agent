# Family (Partner/Spouse) Visa — Financial Requirement

## Minimum income threshold

- **New applications (for applicants who first applied on or after 11 April 2024)**: "your combined income is at least £29,000 a year."
- **Extensions (for applicants who first applied before 11 April 2024)**: the earlier threshold of "your combined income is at least £18,600 a year" still applies.

"Combined income" means the UK-based partner's income together with the applicant's own permitted income sources can be counted together toward the threshold.

## Additional amount per child (extension cases only)

For extensions under the pre-11-April-2024 rules, additional income is required per qualifying child:

- "£3,800 a year for the first child"
- "£2,400 a year for each additional child"

The combined total is capped at £29,000 a year — i.e. the child additions never push the required amount above the new-application threshold.

## Savings as an alternative to income

"You may be able to use your savings instead of income to show you meet the requirement" — gov.uk does not give the exact savings-to-income conversion figure in this excerpt; do not state a specific savings figure without confirming it.

## Exemption

"You do not need to meet a minimum income requirement if your partner gets" a specified disability or carer's benefit (e.g. Armed Forces Independence Payment or a Guaranteed Income Payment under the Armed Forces Compensation Scheme are examples named by gov.uk) — confirm the exact benefits list for a specific case rather than assuming which benefits qualify.
