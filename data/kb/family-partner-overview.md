# Family (Partner/Spouse) Visa — Overview

The UK family visa guide covers several relationship-based routes (partner/spouse, parent, child, adult dependent relative, private life). This knowledge base covers the **partner/spouse route only** — the route for joining a **husband, wife, civil partner, or unmarried partner** — "you need a family visa to live with a family member in the UK for more than 6 months," and you can apply as a partner to live with your "spouse or partner."

## Application fee

- Applying from **outside the UK**: £2,064
- Applying from **inside the UK** (extend/switch): £1,407

## Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS)

Payable in addition to the application fee, scaled to visa length — the guide gives "£2,587.50 if you're staying for 2 years and 6 months" as a worked example (the initial partner visa grant is commonly 2 years 9 months on the 5-year settlement route; confirm the exact IHS total for the specific grant length rather than assuming this example figure applies directly).

## Settlement

Time on this visa can lead to indefinite leave to remain (ILR) via a 5-year route (5 continuous years on a partner family visa) or a 10-year route (10 continuous years, which can combine time on other visas) — see `family-partner-settlement-01` for detail.

See `family-partner-eligibility-01` for who qualifies, `family-partner-financial-requirement-01` for the income/savings threshold, and `family-partner-required-documents-01` for what to provide.
