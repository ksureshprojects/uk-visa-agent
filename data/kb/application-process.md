# Standard Visitor Visa — Application Process

## When to apply
- Applications must be made and a decision received **before travelling** to the UK.
- The **earliest** an applicant can apply is generally **3 months before** the date they intend to travel.

## Steps
1. **Apply online** via the gov.uk application service, selecting the Standard Visitor route and, if desired, a long-term visa validity (2, 5 or 10 years — see `overview.md`).
2. **Prove identity and submit documents**, either:
   - Using the **"UK Immigration: ID Check" app** to verify identity and upload documents digitally (available for many, but not all, nationalities/document types), **or**
   - **Attending an appointment at a visa application centre (VAC)** to provide biometric information (fingerprints and a photograph) and submit supporting documents in person.
3. **UKVI begins processing** the application once identity has been verified and documents submitted, by either of the above routes.
4. **Await decision** — the applicant receives an **email** once a decision has been made, explaining next steps (e.g. how to collect a vignette/passport, or how to access an eVisa/digital status).

## Processing time
- Standard processing: a decision is usually given **within 3 weeks** of completing the identity/document submission step (not from the date of starting the online form).
- **Priority services** are available in most countries for an extra fee, for a faster decision (see `fees-and-processing.md`).

## Long-term visitor visas
- Frequent visitors may apply for a **long-term Standard Visitor visa** valid for **2, 5, or 10 years**, allowing multiple entries throughout that period, **but each individual visit is still capped at a maximum stay of 6 months**, the same as the standard 6-month visa.
- If the applicant is **under 18** at the time of application, a long-term visa's validity is capped so it expires **no later than 6 months after their 18th birthday**.
