# Standard Visitor Visa — Application Process

## When to apply

- Applications must be made, and a decision received, **before travelling** to the UK.
- "The earliest you can apply is **3 months before you travel**."

## Steps

1. **Apply online** via the gov.uk application service, selecting the Standard Visitor route and, if desired, a long-term visa validity (2, 5 or 10 years — see `overview.md`).
2. **Attend an appointment at a visa application centre (VAC)** to give biometric information (fingerprints and a photograph) and submit supporting documents in person — this is the identity/document step confirmed directly on the live gov.uk apply page.
3. **UKVI processes** the application once biometrics and documents have been submitted.
4. **Await decision.**

## Processing time

- "You'll usually get a decision on your visa within **3 weeks**" after completing the biometric appointment and document submission (not from the date of starting the online form).
- A **faster decision** may be available — gov.uk links to a separate "check to see if you can get your visa decision faster" service (see `fees-and-processing.md` for the priority/super priority fee figures, confirmed from that dedicated faster-decision page).

## Long-term visitor visas

- Frequent visitors may apply for a **long-term Standard Visitor visa** valid for **2, 5, or 10 years**, allowing multiple entries throughout that period, but **each individual visit is still capped at a maximum stay of 6 months**, the same as the standard 6-month visa.
- If the applicant is **under 18** at the time of application, a long-term visa's validity is capped so it expires **no later than 6 months after their 18th birthday**.

## Related routes with different fees/validity

- A visitor coming **only for transit** through the UK can instead apply for a **Visitor in Transit visa** (£74.50), rather than the standard Standard Visitor visa.
- Standard Visitor sub-categories for **medical treatment** (up to 11 months) and **academic visits** (up to 12 months) carry their own, higher fees and longer maximum stays than the base 6-month Standard Visitor visa — see `fees-and-processing.md`.

## Extending a stay (from inside the UK)

- A Standard Visitor already in the UK for less than 6 months can, in limited circumstances, apply to **extend their stay** without leaving — e.g. ongoing private **medical treatment** (extendable beyond 6 months without a further limit stated), qualifying **academic** purposes (extendable up to 12 months total), **PLAB test** retakes for medical professionals, or **clinical/dental observer** placements for graduates (up to 18 months total).
- Extension applicants must apply while still in the UK, before their current permission expires, and must still meet the eligibility requirements for their visitor category, with supporting documentation appropriate to the reason for extension (e.g. medical certificates, academic credentials).
- Standard extension decisions typically take **8 weeks**; an optional super priority service (extra £1,000) gives a decision by the next working day (or 2 days over a weekend appointment) — see `fees-and-processing.md` for the extension fee itself.

## Note on identity verification method

This live fetch of the gov.uk apply page did not restate the "UK Immigration: ID Check" smartphone-app option for identity verification (used elsewhere in the UK's digital immigration system for some nationalities/document types) — only the in-person VAC biometric appointment was explicitly described on this page. Applicants should check the specific instructions given during their own online application, since availability of app-based identity verification may vary by nationality and was not independently reconfirmed on this page during this KB refresh.
