# Standard Visitor Visa — Eligibility Criteria

Immigration Rules Appendix V: Visitor covers four visitor types: **Standard Visitor** (tourism, visiting family; 6-month to 10-year visas available), **Marriage/Civil Partnership Visitor** (to marry or give notice), **Transit Visitor** (passing through the UK, up to 48 hours), and **Diplomatic Visa Arrangement (DVA) Visitor** (holders of diplomatic passports, 2-year visas, max 6-month stays per visit). This file covers the Standard Visitor requirements.

## Entry requirements (Appendix V, paragraph V 1)

- Visa nationals must obtain entry clearance (a visa) before arrival, unless a specific exception applies.
- Non-visa nationals may seek entry on arrival at the UK border.
- Applicants intending to marry or register a civil partnership require entry clearance, unless they are "relevant nationals" (an ETA/exemption category).
- Any stay exceeding 6 months requires advance entry clearance — a Standard Visitor cannot be granted permission to stay longer than 6 months on arrival without it.

## Genuine visitor requirement (Appendix V, paragraph V 4.2)

To be granted permission as a Standard Visitor, an applicant must satisfy the decision maker (Home Office caseworker) that they will:

1. **Leave the UK at the end of their visit.**
2. **Not live in the UK for extended periods** through frequent or successive visits, and not make the UK their **main home**.
3. **Seek entry (or stay) only for purposes permitted** under the Visitor route (see `permitted-activities.md`).
4. **Not undertake any prohibited activity** (see `prohibited-activities.md`).
5. Have **sufficient funds** to cover all reasonable costs of the visit — for themselves and any dependants — without working or accessing public funds (see `financial-requirement.md`).

This "genuine visitor" requirement is assessed in detail by caseworkers — see `genuine-visitor-test.md` for how it is evaluated in practice, including travel-history and credibility factors.

## Prohibited activities (Appendix V, paragraphs V 4.4–4.6)

A Standard Visitor must not work, study except as permitted, access non-private medical treatment, or marry/register a civil partnership (unless applying specifically as a Marriage/Civil Partnership Visitor). See `prohibited-activities.md` for the full breakdown.

## Passport and entry clearance

Applicants must also hold a **valid passport or other travel document**, valid for the whole of the intended stay, and, where required, a valid **entry clearance (visa)** for the Standard Visitor route obtained before travel.

Applicants must not fall foul of general grounds for refusal (e.g. criminality, deception, previous UK immigration breaches, prior overstaying) — the guidance explicitly frames the genuine-visitor determination as "distinct and separate from suitability under Part Suitability," meaning suitability is assessed under separate rules, not the Visitor-specific eligibility rules described here.

## Special/variant requirements

Appendix V: Visitor sets out additional or variant requirements for applicants who are: children, studying, receiving medical treatment, donating an organ, academics, or undertaking a Permitted Paid Engagement.

## Age

There is no minimum age. A child can be granted a Standard Visitor visa, but an application on their behalf must be made by a parent or legal guardian, and consent/care arrangements while in the UK may be scrutinised (see the `/standard-visitor/if-youre-under-18` gov.uk sub-page). If a long-term visa (2/5/10 years) is granted to an applicant who is under 18, that visa's validity is capped to expire no later than 6 months after the applicant turns 18.
