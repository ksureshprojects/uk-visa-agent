# Skilled Worker Visa — Application Process

## How to apply

Applicants "apply online for a Skilled Worker visa" and can "save your form and complete it later" partway through.

## Identity verification

One of two routes:

1. The **"UK Immigration: ID Check" app** — scan identity documents while creating or signing into a UKVI account (no in-person appointment needed if this option is available to the applicant's document type/nationality).
2. A **visa application centre appointment** — attend in person to give "fingerprints and photo (biometric information)." Applicants must bring their passport to the appointment and "will be able to collect it on the day" in most cases.

## Processing time

"You'll usually get a decision within 3 weeks" of submitting the online application, proving identity, and providing supporting documents — for applications made from outside the UK. Processing can take longer if documents need verification, an interview is required, or there are personal-circumstance complications (e.g. a criminal record check flag).

A faster (priority) decision service may be available to purchase at the time of application, subject to availability.

## After you apply

Applicants get "an email containing the decision on your application," explaining next steps. Applications can be withdrawn; a fee refund is only available if "UKVI has not started processing your application" yet.

## Before applying

Check the sponsor's certificate of sponsorship details and "which documents you'll need to apply" (see `skilled-worker-required-documents-01`) before starting the online form.
