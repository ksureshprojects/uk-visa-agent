# Skilled Worker Visa — Required Documents

## Always required

- "your certificate of sponsorship reference number - your employer will give you this"
- Evidence of "knowledge of English"
- "a valid passport or other document that shows your identity and nationality"
- Your job title and annual salary
- Your job's occupation code
- "the name of your employer and their sponsor licence number"

## Required depending on circumstances

- Bank statements proving "personal savings to support yourself in the UK" (a maintenance/savings check, separate from the salary requirement)
- Proof of relationship, if bringing a partner or children as dependants
- Tuberculosis test results, if applying from a country on the TB-testing list
- A criminal record certificate for certain roles — specifically named examples include roles in **education, healthcare, therapy, and social services** (e.g. teachers, nurses, social workers)
- "a valid ATAS certificate" for PhD-level research roles in sensitive subject areas
- A UK PhD certificate, or an Ecctis reference number for an equivalent foreign qualification, where the role requires a PhD
- Certified translations of any document not already in English or Welsh

## Prior-residence criminal record certificates

Applicants who have lived outside the UK need a criminal record certificate covering that residence:

- **Under 28**: certificates for any country lived in for 12+ months (in a row) since the age of 18.
- **28 or over**: certificates for any country lived in for 12+ months (in a row) in the last 10 years.
