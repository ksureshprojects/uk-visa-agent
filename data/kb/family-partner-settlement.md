# Family (Partner/Spouse) Visa — Path to Settlement

Applicants can apply for indefinite leave to remain (ILR) via one of three routes, depending on which route their leave was granted under (check "the letter you got from the Home Office when your 'leave to remain' was last approved" to confirm which applies):

- **5-year route**: having "been living in the UK on your family visa as a partner for at least 5 continuous years."
- **2-year route**: having "been living in the UK on your family visa as a partner for at least 2 continuous years" — this shorter route applies to a specific subset of cases (gov.uk does not fully detail the qualifying conditions in this excerpt; confirm eligibility for the 2-year route rather than assuming it applies generally).
- **10-year route**: having "been living in the UK for at least 10 continuous years," which can combine time spent on other visa routes that lead toward settlement, not only the partner family visa itself.

gov.uk does not state the initial partner family visa's standard grant length on this page — confirm the specific grant length from the applicant's own Home Office decision letter rather than assuming a fixed figure.
