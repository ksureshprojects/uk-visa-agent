# Family (Partner/Spouse) Visa — Eligibility

This route is what gov.uk calls the "partner or spouse" family visa — in everyday terms, this is the route for joining a **husband, wife, or unmarried partner** who is in the UK.

## Age

"To apply as a partner, you and your partner both need to be 18 or over."

## Qualifying relationship — one of the following

- "you're in a civil partnership or marriage that's recognised in the UK"
- "you've been living together in a relationship for at least 2 years when you apply" (unmarried/non-civil partners)
- You're engaged/planning to marry or form a civil partnership "within 6 months of arriving" (fiancé(e)/proposed civil partner route)
- A long-distance relationship of at least 2 years where "you cannot live together" — this is still eligible, it doesn't require prior cohabitation

## The UK-based partner (sponsor) must be one of

- A British or Irish citizen
- Settled in the UK (indefinite leave to remain, settled status, or permanent residence)
- An EU/EEA citizen with pre-settled status who arrived in the UK before 1 January 2021
- A Turkish Businessperson or Worker visa holder
- A person with refugee or humanitarian protection status
- A stateless person with permission to stay

## Other requirements

- "You and your partner must intend to live together permanently in the UK after you apply" — the relationship must be genuine and ongoing, not a route being used only to obtain immigration status.
- Applicants must prove "knowledge of English" (this KB does not yet detail the specific accepted evidence for the family route — treat this as a known gap rather than assuming it matches the Student or Skilled Worker routes' rules).
- Applicants must meet the financial requirement (see `family-partner-financial-requirement-01`) unless a specific exemption applies.
