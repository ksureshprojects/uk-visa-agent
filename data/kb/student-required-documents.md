# Student Visa — Required Documents

## Always required

- "a current passport or other valid travel documentation"
- "a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from your course provider"

## Required depending on circumstances

- Proof of sufficient funds to support yourself and pay for your course (see `student-financial-requirement-01`)
- "a valid ATAS certificate if your course and nationality require it" (sensitive research subjects)
- Parental or legal guardian consent, if under 18
- Proof of relationship to your parent or guardian, if under 18
- Tuberculosis test results, if applying from a country on the TB-testing list
- "written consent for your application from your financial sponsor if you've received sponsorship" within the last 12 months

## Applicants under 18

Additional requirements:

- "written consent from both parents or legal guardians (or one parent if they have sole responsibility)" — this consent must cover the visa application itself, living arrangements in the UK, and travel to the UK.
- A copy of the applicant's birth certificate, or another government-issued document showing the parents' names.

gov.uk notes: "You may need to provide additional documents depending on your circumstances" — this list covers the commonly-required items, not every possible case.
