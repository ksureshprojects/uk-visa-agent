# Skilled Worker Visa — Fees and Costs

## Application fee (standard rate)

Applying from **outside the UK**:
- "up to 3 years - £819 per person"
- "more than 3 years - £1,618 per person"

Applying from **inside the UK** (extend/switch):
- "up to 3 years - £943 per person"
- "more than 3 years - £1,865 per person"

## Application fee (Immigration Salary List roles)

A reduced rate applies for roles on the Immigration Salary List:
- "£628 if you're staying for up to 3 years"
- "£1,235 if you're staying for more than 3 years"

## Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS)

"This is usually £1,035 per year" of visa length, paid upfront as part of the application (in addition to the application fee). No healthcare-worker discount or reduced surcharge rate is documented on this page.

## Savings / maintenance requirement

Applicants "usually need to have at least £1,270 available" — "you must have at least £1,270 in your bank account" — unless the sponsor is certifying maintenance on the applicant's behalf (confirm with the specific sponsor's CoS).

## What this means in practice

For a typical sponsored role lasting up to 3 years, the applicant should budget roughly the £819 (or £628, if Immigration Salary List) application fee, plus the IHS at ~£1,035 per year of visa length, plus the £1,270 savings threshold — these are separate costs, not alternatives to one another.
