# Student Visa — Fees and Costs

## Application fee

"£558 to apply for a Student visa from outside the UK" and "£558 to extend or switch to a Student visa from inside the UK" — the same flat fee applies in all three cases (new application, extension, switch).

## Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS)

The IHS is payable as part of the application, in addition to the £558 fee. "How much you pay depends on how long your visa lasts" — confirm the exact per-year IHS rate at the time of application rather than assuming a fixed figure, since it is set separately from the visa application fee itself.

## Related costs (not fees, but budget-relevant)

See `student-financial-requirement-01` for the separate living-cost and course-fee figures that must also be shown as available funds — those are a maintenance requirement, not an application fee, but are often the largest cost component of a Student visa application in practice.
