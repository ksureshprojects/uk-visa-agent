# Family (Partner/Spouse) Visa — Required Documents

## Identity and immigration history

- All names, date of birth, and "your current passport or other valid travel ID"
- Copies of the photo page and any visa/entry stamps in previous passports
- Details of any previous immigration applications made
- Details of any criminal convictions
- National Insurance number, if the applicant has one
- Parents' date of birth and nationality, if applying from outside the UK

## Sponsor's immigration status

- Online proof of immigration status (an eVisa share code), **or**
- A biometric residence permit (BRP), where still usable for the relevant timeframe

## English and finances

- Proof of "knowledge of English"
- "Proof of your finances, if you're not applying on the basis of your private life" (see `family-partner-financial-requirement-01`)

## Financial evidence — acceptable income sources

- "income from employment before tax and National Insurance"
- "income earned from self-employment"
- "cash savings above £16,000"
- "money from a pension"
- "non-work income, for example from property rentals or dividends"

Typical supporting documents: bank statements, 6 months of payslips, or "a letter from an employer, dated and on headed paper."

## Relationship and family evidence

- Details of the current partner, any previous relationships, and any children
- Proof of living arrangements and relationship history (e.g. joint bills, tenancy agreements, correspondence over time)

## Other, if applicable

- Tuberculosis test results
- A certified translation of any document not already in English or Welsh
