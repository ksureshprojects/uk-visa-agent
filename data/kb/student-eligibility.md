# Student Visa — Eligibility

## Age

"You can apply for a Student visa to study in the UK if you're 16 or over." Applicants aged 16-17 need evidence of parental consent when they apply.

## Course / sponsorship requirement

Applicants must have "been offered a place on a course by a licensed student sponsor" — a **Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)** from that sponsor is the formal evidence of this offer (see `student-required-documents-01`).

## Financial requirement

Applicants need "enough money to support yourself and pay for your course — the amount will vary depending on your circumstances." The specific monthly figures (which differ for London vs outside London) are in `student-financial-requirement-01` — do not quote a single flat figure without checking which region applies.

## English language requirement

Applicants must be able to "speak, read, write and understand English," demonstrated via one of:

- A qualifying academic qualification — a GCSE, A level, or Scottish equivalent (National Qualification level 4-5, Higher, or Advanced Higher) in English; a degree from a UK institution; or a degree taught in English from a non-UK institution (with an Ecctis assessment confirming equivalence).
- A **Secure English Language Test (SELT)** from an approved provider, for applicants without a qualifying academic qualification.
- Assessment directly by the Higher Education Provider, in some cases (results must meet the same equivalent standard).

Required proficiency level depends on course level: **CEFR B2** for degree-level study, **CEFR B1** for below-degree-level study.

## English exemptions

Applicants are exempt from proving English separately if they are nationals of certain countries (examples given: Australia, New Zealand, USA, Canada, Ireland — the full list is maintained on gov.uk), already proved their English level in a previous UK visa application, or are on a USA study-abroad programme.
