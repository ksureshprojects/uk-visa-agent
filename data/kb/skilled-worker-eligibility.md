# Skilled Worker Visa — Eligibility

## Sponsorship (the threshold requirement)

You must "work for a UK employer that's been approved by the Home Office" and hold "a 'certificate of sponsorship' (CoS) from your employer with information about the role you've been offered in the UK." A confirmed job offer from a licensed sponsor is mandatory before you can apply — there is no route to apply speculatively without one.

## Eligible occupation

The role you're sponsored for must be on the list of eligible occupations that the Home Office publishes for this route. gov.uk does not restate the full occupation-code list on the general eligibility page — check the specific role's eligibility with the sponsoring employer/its occupation code rather than assuming.

## Salary requirement

Applicants must "be paid a minimum salary — how much depends on the type of work you do and the date you got your CoS." gov.uk does not publish one flat figure on the general eligibility page; the threshold is role- and date-specific (tied to the occupation's "going rate" and the sponsorship date). Do not state a specific salary figure as a general rule — confirm the applicable threshold for the specific role and CoS date instead.

## English language requirement

Applicants "must be able to speak, read, write and understand English" and "usually need to prove your knowledge of English when you apply."

## Other eligibility facts

- Visa validity is up to 5 years before extension is needed.
- Settlement (indefinite leave to remain) is possible after 5 years of continuous residence on this route.
- A partner and children may qualify as dependants.
- Sponsored work authorisation extends to work in UK waters.
