# Student Visa — Overview

## Who it's for

Applicants must be 16 or over and:

- "have been offered a place on a course by a licensed student sponsor"
- "have enough money to support yourself and pay for your course"
- "can speak, read, write and understand English"
- if aged 16-17, have parental consent

Those aged 16-17 studying at an independent school may need a **Child Student visa** instead of the (adult) Student visa.

## Visa duration

Varies by course level and prior UK study, for applicants 18+:

- **Degree-level study**: up to 5 years.
- **Below degree-level study**: up to 2 years.

## Cost

"£558 to apply for a Student visa from outside the UK" — the same £558 figure applies to extending or switching to a Student visa from inside the UK. The Immigration Health Surcharge applies separately (see `student-fees-01`).

## Dependants

"You may be able to bring your partner and children ('dependants')" — eligibility for this depends on course level and funding type (postgraduate research courses of 9+ months are more commonly eligible than shorter/undergraduate courses).

## Work

Students can study and may work as a student union sabbatical officer; further work eligibility depends on the course and term-time status — see `student-work-restrictions-01`. Students cannot be self-employed or work in certain roles (e.g. professional sportsperson/sports coach), and cannot claim public funds (benefits) or pensions.

## When to apply

Apply up to 6 months before the course starts if applying from outside the UK, or up to 3 months before if applying from inside the UK. Typical decision times are "3 weeks" (outside the UK) or "8 weeks" (inside the UK).
