# Student Visa — What You Can and Cannot Do

## Permitted

- Study on the approved course.
- Work as a student union sabbatical officer.
- Work conditionally otherwise — "You may be able to work — how much depends on what you're studying and whether you're working in or out of term-time." gov.uk does not restate an exact weekly-hours figure on this page; the applicable work allowance (if any) is set by the specific course/sponsor and term-time status, so confirm rather than assume a number.

## Not permitted

- Claiming public funds (benefits) and pensions.
- Working in certain jobs — e.g. as a professional sportsperson or sports coach.
- Being self-employed.
- Studying at an academy or a local-authority-funded (maintained) school.

## Dependants and progression

Students "may be able to bring your partner and children ('dependants')" (see `student-overview-01` for the general eligibility caveat on this). After completing the course, options include extending the Student visa or switching to a Graduate visa — the Graduate route itself is outside this knowledge base's coverage.
