# Standard Visitor Visa — Prohibited Activities

Per **Immigration Rules Appendix V, paragraphs V 4.4–4.6**, a Standard Visitor must not:

- **Work** — including employment, self-employment, internships, and direct selling — whether paid or unpaid, and even filling a role or providing short-term/temporary cover for a role within a UK-based organisation, even if payment is made overseas by a non-UK entity. (The one narrow exception is a **Permitted Paid Engagement** — see `permitted-activities.md`.) Payment from UK sources is generally prohibited, except for reasonable expenses, prize money, and specific Permitted Paid Engagements.
- **Study**, except as specifically permitted (short courses up to 6 months and other permitted study arrangements — see `permitted-activities.md`). Longer or non-permitted study requires a Student visa.
- **Access non-private medical treatment** — i.e. NHS treatment beyond what a visitor is separately entitled to; only **private**, self-funded medical treatment is permitted (see `permitted-activities.md`).
- **Marry, register a civil partnership, or give notice** of marriage/civil partnership in the UK — unless applying specifically as a **Marriage/Civil Partnership Visitor**, a distinct visitor sub-category, not the Standard Visitor route.
- **Access public funds** (UK benefits/welfare) — visitors are granted permission subject to a "No Recourse to Public Funds" (NRPF) condition, standard for nearly all temporary UK immigration routes.
- **Live in the UK for extended periods through frequent or successive visits**, or otherwise make the UK their main home (de facto residence) — this overlaps with the genuine visitor test (`genuine-visitor-test.md`) but is also framed as a standalone prohibition.

## Practical note

Because the permitted-activities list is treated as exhaustive under the Rules, any activity a user describes that does not clearly map onto a permitted category (tourism, qualifying business activity, short study, academic visit, private medical treatment, permitted paid engagement, permitted event participation) should be treated as likely prohibited or at least as requiring a different visa route, rather than assumed to be fine by default.

## Four visitor types (context)

Appendix V: Visitor actually defines four visitor categories: **Standard Visitor**, **Marriage/Civil Partnership Visitor**, **Transit Visitor** (up to 48 hours), and **Diplomatic Visa Arrangement (DVA) Visitor**. The prohibitions above apply to the Standard Visitor category specifically; the Marriage/Civil Partnership Visitor category exists precisely to permit what would otherwise be a prohibited activity (marrying) under the Standard Visitor route.
