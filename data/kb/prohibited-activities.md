# Standard Visitor Visa — Prohibited Activities

A Standard Visitor **must not**:

- **Work** in the UK — this includes paid or unpaid work for a UK company, working as a self-employed person, or **filling a role or providing short-term/temporary cover for a role within a UK-based organisation**, whether paid or unpaid, and even if the salary/payment is made overseas by a non-UK entity. (The one narrow exception is a **Permitted Paid Engagement** — see `permitted-activities.md`.)
- **Access public funds** (UK benefits/welfare) — visitors are granted permission subject to a "No Recourse to Public Funds" (NRPF) condition, standard for nearly all temporary UK immigration routes.
- **Study for longer than 6 months**, or study at an institution/on a course that is not accredited for short-term study purposes. Longer study requires a Student visa, not a Standard Visitor visa.
- **Marry, register a civil partnership, or give notice of marriage/civil partnership** in the UK — this specifically requires the separate **Marriage Visitor visa**, not the Standard Visitor visa.
- **Live in the UK for extended periods through frequent or successive visits**, or otherwise make the UK their main home (de facto residence) — this overlaps with the genuine visitor test (`genuine-visitor-test.md`) but is also framed as a standalone prohibition.
- Undertake any activity **outside** the specific list of permitted activities for the Standard Visitor route (i.e. the permitted-activities list is exhaustive, not illustrative — anything not on it is implicitly prohibited unless another visa route specifically allows it).

## Practical note
Because the permitted-activities list is treated as exhaustive under the Rules, any activity a user describes that does not clearly map onto a permitted category (tourism, qualifying business activity, short study, academic visit, private medical treatment, permitted paid engagement, permitted event participation) should be treated as likely prohibited or at least as requiring a different visa route, rather than assumed to be fine by default.
