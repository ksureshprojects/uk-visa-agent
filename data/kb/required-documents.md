# Standard Visitor Visa — Required Supporting Documents

## Always required
- A **valid passport or other travel document**, covering the entire intended stay in the UK. This must be provided both with the application and when travelling.
- Evidence supporting the **genuine visitor test** (purpose of trip, intention to leave — see `genuine-visitor-test.md`) and the **financial requirement** (see `financial-requirement.md`).

## Financial evidence
- **Bank statements** that show the applicant has sufficient funds available, and — importantly — should **detail the origin of the funds** being relied on (i.e. not just a balance, but a statement/transaction history that shows the money is legitimately the applicant's own, from salary, savings accumulation, etc., rather than a single suspicious lump-sum deposit shortly before applying).
- If employed: letters from the employer on **company headed paper**, detailing role, salary, and length of employment, and (if applicable) confirming a leave of absence / approved dates off work for the trip.
- If a student: a letter from the education provider confirming enrolment and any approved leave of absence for the trip dates.

## Invitation / hosting evidence (purpose-dependent)
- **Business event/conference**: a letter of invitation from the **event organiser**.
- **Working with UK-based colleagues**: a letter from the **applicant's own employer** confirming the purpose and arrangements of the visit.
- **Academic visit**: a letter from the applicant's employer outlining sabbatical/exchange dates and the research to be undertaken, **or** a letter from the UK host institution confirming the research/exchange/clinical-practice arrangements.
- **Private medical treatment (including S2 Healthcare Visitors)**: a letter from a **doctor or consultant** that includes (a) details of the condition requiring consultation/treatment, (b) the **estimated cost and likely duration** of treatment, and (c) details of **where** the consultation/treatment will take place.
- **Family/friends visit**: evidence of the relationship to, and the circumstances of, the person being visited in the UK (commonly an invitation letter from the UK-based host, though gov.uk framing emphasises this is supporting evidence rather than a rigid mandatory checklist item).

## Format / validity notes and gaps flagged
- gov.uk's guidance emphasises the **content** bank statements and letters must contain (fund origin; role/salary/dates; treatment cost/duration/location) rather than prescribing a strict numeric **recency window** (e.g. "statements must be dated within the last X months") in the material retrieved for this knowledge base. Several non-official immigration-adviser sites commonly cite informal recency norms (e.g. "last 6 months"), but this could not be confirmed against an official gov.uk statement in this research pass, and should **not** be presented to end users as an official gov.uk rule without further verification.
- The full official document, **"Visiting the UK: guide to supporting documents"**, is the primary authoritative gov.uk source for this topic and should be re-fetched directly (not just via search snippets) when this knowledge base is next refreshed, since direct gov.uk fetches were blocked in this research environment.
- Documents not in English or Welsh should generally be accompanied by a certified translation for any UK visa application; this is a general UK visa-application rule rather than one specific to the Standard Visitor route, and was not independently re-confirmed against a Standard-Visitor-specific gov.uk page in this pass — flagged as a gap.
