# Standard Visitor Visa — Required Supporting Documents

## Always required

- A **valid passport or other travel document**, covering the entire intended stay in the UK. This must be provided both with the application and when travelling.
- Evidence supporting the **genuine visitor test** (purpose of trip, intention to leave — see `genuine-visitor-test.md`) and the **financial requirement** (see `financial-requirement.md`).

## Financial evidence

- **Financial documents showing sufficient funds are available**, which "must clearly show that you have access to the funds," such as: "bank statements which detail the origin of the funds held" or "building society books which detail the origin of the funds held" — i.e. not just a balance, but evidence the money is legitimately the applicant's own.
- Statements/letters **issued more than 1 year before the date of application** are flagged by gov.uk as less useful evidence (see `financial-requirement.md` for the full recency discussion).
- If employed: "a letter from your employer on company headed paper, detailing your role, salary and length of employment," and (if applicable) confirming a leave of absence / approved dates off work for the trip.
- If a student: "a letter from your education provider, on headed paper, confirming your enrolment and leave of absence" for the trip dates.

## Invitation / hosting evidence (purpose-dependent)

- **Business event/conference**: "a letter of invitation from the organiser of the event you are attending."
- **Private medical treatment (including S2 Healthcare Visitors)**: "a letter written by a doctor or consultant" detailing the medical condition, the **estimated cost and likely duration** of treatment, and **where** the consultation/treatment will take place.
- **Marriage/Civil Partnership Visitor**: acceptable evidence includes "an appointment confirmation with a registrar or booking confirmation for your reception" (note: this applies to the separate Marriage/Civil Partnership Visitor category, not the Standard Visitor route itself — see `prohibited-activities.md`).
- **Family/friends visit**: evidence of the relationship to, and circumstances of, the person being visited in the UK — gov.uk frames this as supporting evidence rather than a rigid mandatory checklist item.

## Document quality and format

- Where documents are not in English or Welsh, a **certified translation** should accompany them. The translation must include: "confirmation from the translator that it is an accurate translation of the original document; the date of translation; the translator's full name and signature; the translator's contact details."
- gov.uk recommends: "Wherever possible, digital images should be taken of original documents, not copies."

## Notes

- gov.uk's guidance emphasises the **content** documents must contain (fund origin; role/salary/dates; treatment cost/duration/location; translator attestation) over a rigid, prescriptive checklist — several requirements are framed as "supporting evidence" rather than strict mandatory items for every case.
