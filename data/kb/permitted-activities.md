# Standard Visitor Visa — Permitted Activities

A Standard Visitor may undertake the following activities:

## Tourism and general visiting
- "visit friends and family and / or come to the UK for a holiday" — general tourism/sightseeing and visiting family or friends.
- Participate in educational exchanges with state-funded or independent schools.
- "attend recreational courses (not English Language training) for a maximum of **30 days**."

## Volunteering
- Volunteer for a **registered charity**, provided the volunteering "lasts no more than **30 days in total**." (This resolves an earlier gap in this KB — the cap is confirmed as 30 days, not left unspecified.)

## Business activities
Permitted provided the applicant is not paid by a UK-based organisation or client for the activity itself (i.e. no employment income from a UK source):
- Attend meetings, conferences, seminars, and interviews.
- Give one-off talks/speeches, provided this is **non-commercial and not for profit**.
- Negotiate and sign contracts/deals on behalf of an overseas employer.
- Attend trade fairs for **promotional work only** (not direct selling).
- Conduct site visits and inspections (overseeing delivery of goods/services from a UK company to the visitor's overseas employer).
- Gather employment-related information.

## Academic and research
- Collaborate on research projects related to the applicant's overseas employment.
- Take part in exchange arrangements with UK counterparts.
- Conduct independent research.
- Visits by academics on sabbatical, or undertaking research, with evidence of the arrangement (e.g. an employer's letter confirming a sabbatical, or a UK host institution's letter confirming a research/exchange arrangement).

## Study
- Study for **up to 6 months**, subject to specified requirements (accredited institution, etc. — unless a distance-learning course, which is treated differently).

## Medical treatment
- Receive **private medical treatment** (self-funded), subject to additional requirements — including as an "S2 Healthcare Visitor" (a specific sub-category for those with confirmed, funded treatment).

## Permitted Paid Engagements (PPE)
- A **Permitted Paid Engagement** is a distinct sub-category: an expert may come to the UK to do a **paid** engagement (e.g. give a lecture, examine students, perform, or take part in a competition/event) if they have a written invitation from a UK-based organisation or client, and the engagement is directly relevant to the visitor's expertise, profession and qualifications in their home country.
- The engagement must be completed **within 30 days of the visitor's entry to the UK** (note: this is more precisely "30 days," rather than the looser "within the first month" phrasing used in earlier KB versions).

## Creative and sporting activities
- Perform as an artist or entertainer.
- Participate in sporting events, trials, or competitions.
- Appear in film/media productions.

## Other
- Transiting through the UK.
- School exchange programme participation (see Tourism section above).

## Note on scope
The categories above reflect the live gov.uk guidance for "Immigration Rules Appendix Visitor: Permitted Activities," cross-checked against the `gov.uk/standard-visitor` overview page. As with any Rules-derived summary, an activity a user describes that does not clearly map onto one of these categories should be treated as likely requiring a different visa route rather than assumed permitted by default (see `prohibited-activities.md`).
