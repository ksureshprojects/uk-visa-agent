# Standard Visitor Visa — Permitted Activities

A Standard Visitor may come to the UK to do the following (this is not an application-by-application exhaustive list — see the gap note below):

## Tourism and general visiting
- General tourism / sightseeing.
- Visiting family or friends.

## Business activities
Permitted **provided the applicant is not paid by a UK-based organisation or client for the activity itself** (i.e. no employment income from a UK source):
- Attending meetings, interviews, conferences and seminars.
- Getting work-related training, if the applicant is employed overseas and the specific training is **not available in their home country**.
- Giving a one-off or short series of talks/speeches, provided this is **not for profit** and not at a commercial event.
- Overseeing the delivery of goods/services provided by a UK company to the visitor's overseas employer/organisation (i.e. site visits, quality checks, contract oversight).
- Negotiating and signing deals/contracts on behalf of an overseas employer.
- Attending trade fairs (for promotional work only, not direct selling).

## Study
- Short courses of study of **no more than 6 months**, at an accredited UK institution (unless a distance-learning course, which is treated differently).
- Certain course-related activities at a UK higher education institution, such as short research placements or an elective as part of an overseas degree course.

## Academic visits
- Visits by academics on sabbatical, on an exchange, or undertaking research, provided evidence is given of the arrangement (e.g. an employer's letter confirming a sabbatical, or a UK host institution's letter confirming a research/exchange arrangement).

## Medical treatment
- Receiving **private medical treatment** (self-funded), including as an "S2 Healthcare Visitor" (a specific sub-category for those with confirmed, funded treatment).

## Permitted paid engagements
- A **Permitted Paid Engagement (PPE)** is a distinct sub-category within the Standard Visitor route: an expert may come to the UK for up to 6 months to do a **paid** engagement (e.g. give a lecture, examine students, perform, or take part in a competition/event) if:
  - They have a **written invitation from a UK-based organisation or client** for a specific, pre-arranged event.
  - The engagement is **directly relevant to the visitor's expertise, profession and qualifications** in their home country.
  - The engagement must be carried out **within the first month** of the visit (even though the overall permitted stay can be up to 6 months).

## Other
- Attending or performing at a permitted arts, entertainment or sporting **event** as a performer/competitor/artist under specific visitor sub-rules.
- Short-term volunteering with a registered charity (subject to a time cap under the Rules; the exact cap could not be confirmed — see gap note).
